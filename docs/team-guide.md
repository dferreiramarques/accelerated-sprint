# Team guide

For anyone taking part in a sprint. Two minutes to read.

## What this is

The sprint room is the shared screen for the day. It tells you **what we are working on, which block is active, how long is left, and what the facilitator wants you to know**. It is not where you do the work and nothing you do in it is graded.

You work with your squad on your own laptop (Gemini, NotebookLM, paper, sticky notes). At the end, each team presents on the Teams call.

## Joining

1. Open the link the facilitator shared, or go to the site and choose **Run my Sprint!**.
2. Enter the 6-character sprint code (letters and digits, no I, O, 0 or 1).
3. Pick your team or type a new team name, and add your first name or initials (optional).
4. You land in the lobby until the facilitator starts.

If you refresh the page or lose connection, you come back to your team automatically on the same browser. On a different device, join again and pick the same team.

## During the sprint

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active block** | What we are doing now: steps, expected output, and AI prompts you can copy |
| **Timer** | Same clock for everyone, kept by the server. Pauses and time added by the facilitator show up for all |
| **Banner and a beep** | A message from the facilitator, or time is up |

Buttons you have:

- **Mark our team done**: tell the facilitator your team finished this block. Click again to undo.
- **We need help**: raises a flag the facilitator sees. It clears when the next block starts.
- **Message**: write to the facilitator. Other teams do not see it.

The AI prompts are starting points. Placeholders like `[PROBLEM]` and `[KPI]` are filled from the brief; anything in square brackets that is still left (for example `[paste steps]`) is for you to complete.

## Ground rules

These come from the brief and are shown in the app:

- Think first, AI second.
- Together alone: ideas made individually, shared as a group.
- The timer rules; side topics go to the parking lot.
- The decider decides.
- **Anonymised data only**, corporate Gemini and NotebookLM accounts.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to type my answers into the app?** No. The app has no forms for results. You present on the call.

**Who can see what I send?** Messages you send go to the facilitator only. Other teams and the room screen never see them. The facilitator sees the names you type; your own team sees them too, other teams do not, and the room screen never shows names, only team names.

**Can someone outside the sprint get in?** Only with the sprint code, and repeated wrong guesses are blocked. Treat the code like a meeting link: share it with participants only. See [Data, security and reliability](data-and-reliability.md).

**The timer hit zero and nothing happened.** That is by design. The facilitator decides whether to extend, move on or stop.

**My screen looks frozen.** Refresh the page. Your team and the current block come back. If the page does not load at all, tell the facilitator; they have a fallback (see the facilitator guide).

**Can I use it on my phone?** Yes, the layout is built to work on a phone as well as a laptop.

**What happens to the data after the sprint?** The facilitator can delete it at any time, and after 90 idle days it is archived and its code stops working. Keep briefs free of personal or confidential data.
